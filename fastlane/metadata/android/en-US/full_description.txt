Ficelle is a small notes app that opens in a moment and uses little memory. Notes are Markdown, with checklists, tables, callouts and images you place like in a word processor. Every note keeps its earlier versions.

Your notes stay on your own devices. Your phone and your computer sync directly on the same Wi-Fi, with no account and no server. Notes can be encrypted with a password, and backups go to a folder you choose.

Ficelle also runs on Linux and Windows, and as a web version in any browser. Its source is on GitHub under the AGPL.
